About Us

We opened our first pizzeria in 2004 in the burgeoning Queen Anne neighborhood of Seattle, Washington. Two years earlier we had returned from a family trip to Naples, Italy during which we tasted the best pizza we had ever eaten. The idea to bring this amazing pizza back to Seattle came to life on the Alitalia flight back to the US. We flew in a team of masons from Naples to build a gargantuan wood-fired oven, who brought with them bricks rich in Vesuvian ash…
